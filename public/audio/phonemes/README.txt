Lexi phoneme audio library

Add validated human-recorded MP3 clips here using:
b.mp3, ch.mp3, l.mp3, m.mp3, p.mp3, r.mp3, s.mp3, sh.mp3,
short-a.mp3, short-i.mp3, short-o.mp3

Record the phoneme itself, not the letter name. For stop consonants, do not add
a schwa: /p/, not "puh". Keep speaker, volume, microphone distance, and trimming
consistent.

Lexi automatically prefers these recordings. Until a recording exists, the
new phoneme service plays one browser-speech cue at a time so Mystery Words no
longer sends strings like "s... l... o... p" to speechSynthesis.
